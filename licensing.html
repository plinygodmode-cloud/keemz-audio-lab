Licensing

What you can do with the sound.

Every pack sold in the sample store is a licence, not a transfer of ownership. The terms below are the standard terms for Keemz Audio Lab sample packs. Where a product page states something different, the product page wins.

1. What a standard purchase grants

  • A non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free licence to use the included sounds in your own music productions and releases.
  • Use in commercial releases, streaming distribution, sync within your own content, and live performance.
  • Editing, pitching, processing and layering the sounds as part of a new work.
  • The licence is granted to the individual buyer and does not expire.

2. What it does not grant

  • You may not resell, sublicense, share, upload or redistribute the raw files — in whole or as a sample pack, loop library, template, or “free download” bundle.
  • You may not use the sounds as the main content of a sample-based product, a stock audio item, or a competing sound library.
  • You may not register the raw loops or one-shots with a content-ID system or claim authorship of them.
  • No exclusivity is granted: the same sounds may be licensed to other artists.

3. Third-party material

Some packs may contain one-shots or loops from third-party instruments, plugins or sample sources. Those sounds are included only where their own licence allows redistribution inside a sample pack. If a pack contains such material, the product page says so. Do not assume every pack is cleared for every use — ask before a major campaign.

5. Credit and registration

Credit is appreciated, not required for packs. For commissioned production, splits and credits are agreed in writing before delivery. You are responsible for registering your own releases with your distributor or collection society.

6. Delivery, refunds and replacement

Packs are delivered as private, expiring download links after the payment provider confirms payment. Because delivery is instant and irreversible, digital packs are non-refundable once downloaded. If a file is corrupt, missing, or the link expired before you could save it, contact the studio and a replacement is sent at no cost.

No “100% royalty-free” shortcuts. Nothing here is advertised as free of obligations beyond the terms written above. If your project needs clearance for television, film, games or advertising, ask first and get it in writing.

These terms describe the default licence for this store. They are not legal advice, and a lawyer should review them for your territory before high-volume sales.
